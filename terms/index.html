Verification Terms

These terms describe the rules for using Keltec PMC's Roblox account verification service.

1. Use of the service

By starting verification, you authorize the verification service to obtain the Roblox account information made available through the verification flow and to use that information for Keltec PMC verification and related group-management purposes.

2. Account ownership

You should only verify a Roblox account that you control. You must not use the service to impersonate another person or to obtain access to another person's account or private information.

3. Roblox credentials

The verification service does not ask you to provide your Roblox password. Authentication and authorization are handled through the applicable Roblox flow.

4. Verification records

A successful verification may create a record connecting your Roblox account with your Keltec PMC membership or verification status. These records may be used for ranking, membership administration, and other Keltec PMC management functions.

5. Service availability

The verification service is provided for Keltec PMC's verification workflow and may be changed, suspended, or discontinued as the underlying systems are updated.

6. Contact

Questions concerning these terms or the verification process can be directed to me@apleut.dev.
