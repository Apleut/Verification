Verification Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information the Keltec PMC Roblox verification service may receive, why it is used, and how it relates to verification.

Information received

The verification flow may provide information associated with the Roblox account being verified, such as the account's Roblox user ID, username, and verification or authorization information returned by Roblox. The exact information available depends on the authorization flow used by the service.

How information is used

  • To confirm that a Roblox account completed the verification flow.
  • To associate the verified Roblox account with a Keltec PMC member.
  • To support Roblox group ranking and membership-management functions.
  • To prevent duplicate or misleading verification records.
  • To troubleshoot verification failures and maintain the service.

Information we do not request

The verification service does not require your Roblox password. Do not provide your password, security codes, or other credentials to a person claiming to operate this verification page.

Sharing

Verification information is used for Keltec PMC's verification and group-management purposes. Information may be transmitted to service providers or infrastructure used to operate the verification system when necessary to provide that service. It is not intended to be used for unrelated advertising or sale of personal information.

Retention and deletion

Verification records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain Keltec PMC membership and ranking records, investigate abuse, and operate the verification system. Requests about a verification record can be sent to me@apleut.dev.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the verification service or its data practices change. The date below indicates the latest revision.
